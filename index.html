AI Gym Mentor & Fitness Logger

A localized workout logger and progress analyzer designed to bypass bloated, cloud-dependent subscription apps.

Traditional lifting logs require constant internet connection, sell tracking logs to third parties, or lack personalized insights on progressive overload intervals.
Built an offline-first Flutter application structured around local SQLite schemas. Added a local, offline cache module utilizing the Gemini API to analyze monthly workouts, mapping hypertrophy curves and providing structured gym recommendations.
Successfully tested with a local group of 15 gym enthusiasts, resulting in a 30% speedup in workout logging overhead and consistent progression tracking.

Python SQL Pandas

Private Portfolio & Wealth Tracker

A local parser and analytical rebalancer that visualizes financial assets and tax efficiency without cloud integrations.

Indian retail investors must log accounts across multiple stock brokers, mutual fund platforms, and banking portals, exposing delicate transactional files to third-party sync apps.
Developed a Python validation pipeline utilizing PDF parsing scripts to extract CAS files. This loads statements into SQLite databases, building local rebalancing recommendations based on tax brackets and risk levels.
Consolidates total family wealth calculations in under 2 minutes with absolute data isolation, streamlining tax planning before the fiscal year-end.
