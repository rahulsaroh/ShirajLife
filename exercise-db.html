Overview

Free Exercise DB is an open-source, developer-friendly database created to solve the fragmentation of fitness data. Built to power health and fitness applications, workout trackers, and AI training coaches without vendor lock-in or recurring API subscription fees.

Each movement is mapped with normalized anatomical classifications, equipment constraints, difficulty tiers, and step-by-step execution cues.

Key Features

  • Granular Anatomical Tagging: Every entry specifies primary agonist, synergist, and stabilizer muscle groups.
  • Equipment Taxonomy: Categorized by Barbell, Dumbbell, Cable, Machine, Kettlebell, Bodyweight, and Bands.
  • Media Assets & Thumbnails: Accompanied by demonstration assets and execution animation frames.
  • Zero-Dependency Import: Ready-to-use structured JSON schemas compatible with SQLite, PostgreSQL, Firestore, and Pandas.
