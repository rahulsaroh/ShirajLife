Case Study 01 Flutter + SQLite RFID Hardware

goJim Management OS

Bottleneck: Gym turnstiles stalled during Wi-Fi drops; manual Excel billing leaked renewals.
Architecture: Local-first SQLite queue + deterministic RFID debounce + background sync.
Edge Case: Double-tap turnstile bursts & mid-month plan freezes reconciled idempotently.
Result: 140+ daily check-ins at <15ms latency · ₹2.8L+/mo automated billing.

Case Study 02 Zero-Cloud WASM FinTech

AI Finance Manager (CAS Engine)

Bottleneck: Retail investors refuse uploading password-locked NSDL/CDSL PDFs to third-party servers.
Architecture: In-browser PDF decryption + Newton-Raphson XIRR solver + FIFO tax lot matcher.
Edge Case: Multi-folio ISIN mergers, dividend reinvestments & FY26 STCG/LTCG grandfathering.
Result: 100% offline parsing in <50ms · Zero bytes transmitted off-device.

Quarters Allotment & Audit OS
