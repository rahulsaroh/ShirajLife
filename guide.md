# goJim B2B Dashboard User Guide

Welcome to the **goJim Premium Gym Management Dashboard**! This comprehensive tool allows you to seamlessly manage your entire gym operations, from member tracking to trainer assignments and CRM. 

This guide outlines the features available and how to effectively utilize them.

---

## 1. Role Switching
At the bottom of the left sidebar, you will find a **Role Switcher** widget. This allows you to view the dashboard from different perspectives based on your staff permissions:
- **Gym Owner**: Full access to all top-level tabs (Overview, Classes, Leads CRM, POS, Settings, etc.).
- **Trainer**: Restricted view tailored to managing their specifically assigned clients, training schedules, and direct messages.
- **Student/Member**: Restricted view for a gym member to view their active plans, upcoming classes, metrics, and rate their assigned trainer.

---

## 2. Gym Owner View

### 2.1 Overview Dashboard
The default screen upon logging in. Here you will find:
- **Quick Actions**: Shortcuts to "Enroll New Member", "Add Trainer", and "Broadcast Message".
- **Key Metrics**: High-level KPIs such as Total Members, Check-ins Today, Total Revenue (in ₹), and Active Trainers.
- **Activity Log**: A chronological list of recent events (e.g., check-ins, sales, membership renewals).

### 2.2 Classes & Schedule
*(If activated)* Allows you to define daily class schedules, assign trainers to specific time slots, and monitor class capacity.

### 2.3 Leads CRM (Customer Relationship Management)
The CRM allows you to track potential new members through a Kanban-style pipeline:
- **Pipeline Stages**: `New Leads` → `Trial Booked` → `Trial Done` → `Converted to Member`.
- **Capture Lead**: Use the form on the left side to input prospect details. You can record:
  - Name and Telephone (Required)
  - Email (Optional)
  - Important Dates (Enquiry date, Trial date, Trial Done date, Conversion date).
- **Managing Leads**: Move leads through the pipeline stages by clicking the corresponding action buttons (e.g., "Book Trial 📅", "Mark Attended ✅", "Convert to Member 🏆") on their cards. 

### 2.4 Machinery & Equipment
Manage your gym's physical assets. Keep track of:
- Equipment Name & Type
- Last Serviced Date
- Maintenance Status (Active vs. Needs Repair)

### 2.5 Pro-Shop POS
Manage retail transactions for supplements, merchandise, or drinks sold at the front desk. Keep track of stock and add sales to the day's revenue.

---

## 3. Trainer Management

### 3.1 Adding a Trainer
Click on **Add Trainer Profile** to onboard a new staff member. You can define their specialization (e.g., Powerlifting, Yoga) and assign them a profile.

### 3.2 Managing Trainer Clients
On the Trainer's card, you can see how many students are assigned to them (e.g., `4+ Clients`). **Clicking on this number** will open a detailed modal showing exactly which members are under their supervision, along with their contact details.

---

## 4. Member / Student Management

### 4.1 Enrolling a New Member
Click **Enroll New Member** to open the intake form. Fill out the member's details and **assign them to a specific Trainer**. You can also add **Custom Notes** regarding their fitness goals or health conditions.

### 4.2 Rating a Trainer (Student View)
When switched to the **Student Role**, a member can see their assigned coach. They have the ability to click the **⭐️ Rate Trainer** button to submit feedback, leave a 1-to-5 star rating, and write comments. This helps management maintain high training standards.

---

## 5. Front Desk Kiosk Mode
On the bottom of the sidebar, the **Kiosk Mode** button launches a self-service check-in screen. Members can type their phone number or scan a barcode to instantly check into the facility, updating the "Live Activity" log automatically!
